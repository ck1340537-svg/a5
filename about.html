Our story

About Crochet Bounty

An independent guide for people who love handmade bags and the skilled, patient craft behind them.

How it began

A summer market and a single bag

It started at a summer market, with a crochet tote that had taken its maker nearly a week to finish. Every stitch was even, the handles were wrapped in soft leather, and the lining had a hidden pocket. It cost more than a factory-made bag, and it was worth every penny.

That conversation with the maker sparked a fascination with fibres, stitches and the people who keep this craft alive. Crochet Bounty is where we share what we have learned.

Celebrate the maker

We believe handmade work deserves recognition, fair pay and a little more attention.

Independent

We don’t sell bags, patterns or kits, and we don’t accept payment for coverage.

Buy once, keep long

We encourage choosing well-made bags and caring for them so they last for years.

How we write

Rooted in the craft

Our guides draw on conversations with crocheters and weavers, fibre references and hands-on experience with the materials we write about. We aim to be accurate about fibres and honest about what each one can and can’t do.
