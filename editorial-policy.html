Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Crochet Bounty guide.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want readers and makers alike to trust Crochet Bounty. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, gifted products or affiliate commissions for coverage, and we do not promote specific brands. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

Our guides draw on fibre references, conversations with crocheters and weavers, and hands-on experience. When material names or techniques vary between regions or makers, we say so.

Respect for makers

We aim to describe handmade work accurately and respectfully, and to encourage fair recognition of the time and skill involved. We avoid making claims about specific makers’ practices without verification.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and are described with accurate alternative text. Stitch swatches and interactive tools are created by us as general illustrations.
