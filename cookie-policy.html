Cookie Policy

How Crochet Bounty uses cookies and how to control them.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Crochet Bounty uses cookies and similar technologies on www.crochetbounty.com. Please read it with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help sites remember your preferences and understand how pages are used. Some are set by us and some by third-party services.

Cookies we use

Essential

We store your cookie-notice choice in your browser’s local storage so the notice does not reappear. This is required for the site to work as intended.

Analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to measure visits and popular guides in aggregate. They typically last up to two years.

Advertising

If ads appear, Google and other vendors may set cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites, and to measure performance. Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

Use our cookie notice to accept all cookies or essential ones only, or manage cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some features.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Updates

We may update this policy if our tools or legal requirements change. The date above shows the current version.
