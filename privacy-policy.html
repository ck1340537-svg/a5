Privacy Policy

How Crochet Bounty collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Crochet Bounty (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes www.crochetbounty.com, an independent guide to handmade crochet and woven bags. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Information you share

When you contact us, we receive your name, email address, chosen topic and message. When you subscribe to The Loop Letter, we receive your email address. We use these details only to reply or to send the newsletter. We sell nothing and never ask for payment information.

Automatic information

We automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and visit times. The stitch explorer and style finder run in your browser and your answers are not sent to us. Cookie details are in our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To reply to questions and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides readers enjoy and improve them.
  • To keep the website secure and free from spam.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the website is used. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and time on site, processed by Google under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display ads from Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with providers who help operate the website, such as hosting and email services, or where the law requires.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data follows our Google Analytics retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on your location, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request; every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has shared personal data with us.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No online system is fully secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The date above shows the current version.
